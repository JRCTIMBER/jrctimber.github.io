    Local trees, never imported

    Josh Cook started JRC Timber in 2019 to give surplus local trees a second life. He runs the business from Great Wigborough, with Phil helping in the yard.

    We work with farmers, landowners and tree surgeons around Essex, and we never buy timber from abroad. Each tree is cut here on our sawmill and turned into boards, beams, posts or planks to your specification, or into one-off live-edge slabs for tables, worktops, shelves and resin projects.

    How do I reserve a piece?

    Tap Reserve on any piece in the shop. WhatsApp opens with the piece number already in the message, so we know exactly which one you mean. You can also call us.

    Can I come and see the timber?

    Yes. Visits are by appointment, 7 days a week. Call or message to arrange a time. We're on Layer Road, Great Wigborough, Colchester CO5 7RP.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Pieces up to 80 × 40cm are sent by tracked delivery for £5, and up to 1.2m × 50cm for £10. For bigger pieces, collect from the yard or send us your postcode for a delivery quote. See delivery & returns.

    What does live edge or waney edge mean?

    The natural edge of the tree is left on the board, so its outline follows the shape of the trunk. No two pieces are the same.

    What do "pippy" and "character" mean?

    Pippy timber has clusters of small knots, or pips, that give a speckled look. It's prized in oak and maple. Character timber keeps its knots, splits and grain features rather than having them cut out.

    Is the timber dried?

    Each piece in the shop says whether it's air dried or kiln dried. If you're making furniture for indoors, ask us about the moisture content of the piece you like.

    Can you flatten or plane a slab I buy?

    Yes. Our CNC flattens slabs up to 3m long and 2m wide dead level, and our planer takes pieces up to 800mm wide, including resin-filled ones. We can also cut a piece to size. Just mention it when you reserve.

    Can I bring my own logs to be milled?

    Yes. Our Wood-Mizer LX250 takes logs up to 1.4m (55in) in diameter and 4m long. Send us photos of your logs with their rough length and diameter on WhatsApp and we'll talk you through it.

    Do you make tables to order?

    Yes. We make dining tables, coffee tables and one-off pieces like our engraved railway bench. Send us your ideas and rough sizes.

    How do I look after a live-edge table?

    Wipe up spills quickly, use mats under hot dishes, and keep it away from radiators and strong direct sun. Wood moves a little with the seasons, which is normal. Oiled finishes like a fresh coat now and then. Ask us which finish yours has.
