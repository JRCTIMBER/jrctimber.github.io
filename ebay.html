How to upload

  1. Download the file above.
  2. On eBay go to Seller Hub → Reports → Uploads, click Upload template and choose the file.
  3. Check the results report eBay gives you for any errors.
  4. In the stock manager, tick Listed on eBay on each piece that went up, so it isn't added again.

Sold on eBay? Use "Sold or reserved a piece" in the stock manager. Sold elsewhere? End the listing in the eBay app.
